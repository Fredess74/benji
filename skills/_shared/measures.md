# Measure library

Order: **free operating changes → low-cost controls & lighting → envelope (air sealing, insulation) → equipment /
electrification → on-site generation.** Envelope before heat-pump sizing.

| Measure | Fits | Usually approves | Needs to estimate | Method (formulas.md) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thermostat schedule / set-back | all | whoever controls the thermostat | heating gas split, current schedule | set-back |
| Closing / away routine (lights, HVAC, ovens, signs, PCs, space heaters) | all | occupant | hours, what runs | walk-through; W × h if known |
| Refrigeration care (gaskets, coils, night covers, set-points) | food, grocery | equipment owner | equipment list | no estimate without metering |
| Hot water: safe set-point, leaks, low-flow pre-rinse valves | all | owner/landlord | heater type, fuel | qualitative unless flow known |
| LED lighting | all | renter: bulbs yes, fixtures landlord | count, W old/new, hours, quote | lighting |
| Programmable / smart thermostat | all | renter: ask landlord | thermostat type, price | set-back + price |
| Air sealing / weatherstripping | all | owner (removable kits: renter) | assessment | assessment only |
| Insulation | owner | owner | assessment | assessment only |
| Heat pump (space) | owner | owner | heating split, existing efficiency, seasonal COP from proposal, electrical check | heat pump |
| Heat pump water heater / induction | owner | owner | spec sheet, usage, electrical check | spec-based or qualitative |
| Solar PV | owner with roof | owner | site assessment | assessment only (Benji does not size PV) |
| No-cost energy assessment | all | the person booking (tenants may need consent) | none | finds the measures |

## Electrical check before any new electric load
Collect with source + date: service amps/volts, main breaker, free breaker spaces, nameplate input of the new
equipment. **Never** add breaker ratings to infer spare capacity, read wiring from drawings, or say capacity is
available. Ask a licensed electrician: load calculation result, panel/service upgrade need and cost, circuits and
permits, who must sign off (tenants).
