# Report

## Steps
1. Ask who reads it and what they decide:
   | Reader | Length | Emphasis |
   |---|---|---|
   | Owner | 1-2 pages | next steps, cash now, tasks |
   | Landlord (from a tenant) | 1 page, friendly | the problem in their terms, who pays/saves, small asks, owner programs |
   | Board / partner | 2 pages | options, trade-offs, budget, timeline |
   | Lender | 1 page + tables | cost, cash flow, quotes, estimated savings (labelled) |
   | Contractor | scope sheet | facts, equipment, scope, what the quote must include |
2. Sections: summary (3 lines) · what we found · options · money (confirmed vs hoped-for) · programs (status +
   source) · plan · assumptions & sources · measured results (or "none yet").
3. First line: date and the bill period used. Label every number. Mask account numbers. If findings are STALE,
   refresh first or put "OUT OF DATE" at the top.

## Output
Markdown the user can copy or print; save as `documents/report-<reader>-<date>.md`. Reports are dated snapshots -
write a new one rather than editing an old one.

## Memory
The report file + `log.md` line.
