# Positioning

**Benji is an AI energy assistant that helps people and small businesses investigate avoidable energy spending, choose feasible improvements, and navigate programs such as Mass Save — inside an existing Claude session or a shared workflow with other agents.**

Hackachusetts 2026 · TRACK 3 — CLIMATE TECH · Cleaner, more affordable buildings.

The concrete problem is the gap between an expensive electricity/heating bill and a useful, affordable action. The bill alone does not explain the cause; an upgrade may need upfront cash and landlord consent; program participation may require a provider check, documents and pre-approval. Benji joins these steps and preserves the record for follow-through. A high bill alone does not establish waste.

The intended outcome is less avoidable consumption and a clearer route to assistance. Start with a conversation; add shared memory and a human dashboard when ongoing coordination is useful. Hardware and autonomous monitoring are not prerequisites. Improving program effectiveness means helping users reach the right program and complete the next verified step; it is not a claim of measured program-level impact.

| User | Job |
|---|---|
| Resident or renter | Understand bills/comfort; separate controllable steps from work needing the owner |
| Small business | Investigate use, compare feasible options, prepare an assessment or landlord discussion |
| Owner or manager | Organize evidence, compare appropriate peers and cost/carbon trade-offs, track work |

General assistants can also use tools and memory. Benji's distinctive combination is the explicit energy procedure, references, confirmation rules, structured per-place record and calculation checks. Its incremental advantage must be tested on matched cases. Describe the product as reusable skills and workflows, not an independently trained model or a proven autonomous energy manager.

For scale, the hypothesis is reusable skills and local source adapters. For climate impact, it is more completed, useful improvements. Neither adoption nor field savings has been established.
