# Positioning

**Benji brings energy-management skills to the assistant you already use, turning building information into an affordable next action and an inspectable plan.**

Hackachusetts 2026 · TRACK 3 — CLIMATE TECH · Cleaner, more affordable buildings.

Help exists, but connecting bills, program conditions, available cash and approval responsibilities can be difficult. Benji aims to reduce that coordination work and preserve decisions for the next conversation.

| User | Job |
|---|---|
| Resident or renter | Understand bills/comfort; separate controllable steps from work needing the owner |
| Small business | Investigate use, compare feasible options, prepare an assessment or landlord discussion |
| Owner or manager | Organize evidence, compare appropriate peers and cost/carbon trade-offs, track work |

General assistants can also use tools and memory. Benji's proposed distinction is the explicit energy procedure, references, confirmation rules, structured per-place record and calculation checks. Its incremental advantage must be tested on matched cases.

For scale, the hypothesis is reusable skills and local source adapters. For climate impact, it is more completed, useful improvements. Neither adoption nor field savings has been established.
