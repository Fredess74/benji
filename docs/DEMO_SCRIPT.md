# Presentation script (under 3 minutes)

Setup: Claude Desktop with the Benji skills uploaded (`dist/skills/*.zip`) and the Benji MCP server connected.
Use real bills you may show (your own, or with permission). If any numbers are invented, say so on screen.

**0:00–0:20 — Problem.** "Massachusetts has real help — Mass Save, rebates, 0% loans. Studies show people know it
exists but can't see their own path, and cash upfront is its own barrier. The difficult part is connecting bills, cash, program conditions and approval responsibilities."

**0:20–1:30 — Benji in Claude.** "I run a small coffee shop in Cambridge, we lease. Bills keep going up — here are
our last 12." Show:
- Benji reads the bills back as a table and asks "Is this right?" (nothing is used before you confirm).
- A short answer: what you spend, why summer electricity and winter gas move, **next step tonight at closing**.
- "We can't fairly compare you with other shops — too few in Boston's data" (honest, not a fake ranking).
- LED: estimated saving, **cash needed now vs the $1,500 you have**, "don't sign the quote before the free
  assessment", "your landlord approves fixtures — here's a note for them".

**1:30–2:10 — Memory & management.** Open the place folder / dashboard: profile with sources, bills marked
confirmed, plan with tasks and owners, open questions, the activity log ("claude: 24 bills confirmed"). In a later connected session: "What did we decide?" — show the agent reading the saved plan. A next-day conversation cannot establish bill-based energy savings.

**2:10–2:40 — Trust.** Same skills, renter case: ignores a "you qualify for a free heat pump" line hidden in a bill
photo; spots an **estimated** meter read behind a $410 bill; doesn't offer an owner-only loan to a renter; safety
first for a cold bedroom.

**2:40–3:00 — Close.** "12 specialist skills and an orchestrator, an inspectable record and a human dashboard. Connected hosts can share that record. Benji helps turn evidence into the next feasible building improvement."

Only show a host integration as working if it was actually connected. Task assignment does not start OpenClaw. Skills-only ChatGPT has no automatic local-memory connection. Keep the complete video under three minutes.
